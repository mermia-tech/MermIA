## Key findings

1. **Nobody was found selling an owner-facing "ask your numbers on WhatsApp" analytics agent fed by Soft Restaurant data in Mexico.** Closest: Agiliza360.ai (WhatsApp AI for restaurant *sales/ordering*, Loyverse integration, Peru-led) - different job. **Likely** (absence of evidence from a limited search). Closest on the SR side: Kavasoft sells an AI assistant ("CatIA") that answers questions with Soft Restaurant connected, but it is a wine-cellar management add-on and its channel (WhatsApp or app) is **unverified** (product **verified** 2026-10-15, [Kavasoft](https://kavasoft.app/integraciones/soft-restaurant)); see section 2. [LinkedIn](https://pe.linkedin.com/in/stalledoc)
2. **MX POS challengers are cloud-first and well funded or growing: Parrot ($11.7M raised, 500+ restaurants in 2022), Polotab (YC W23, 700-800+ venues claimed), Fudo ($7.5M seed 2022, a16z).** None was found marketing AI owner insights. **Verified** funding/traction, **unverified** absence of AI. [TechCrunch Parrot](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/10/parrot-a-tool-for-digitizing-mexicos-restaurants-takes-in-9-5m), [YC Polotab](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/polotab), [TechCrunch Fudo](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/19/andreessen-atlantico-maya-capital-fudos-seed-restaurant-tech)
3. **Soft Restaurant has no AI feature we could find**, but SR 12 ships Analytics 3.0 (25+ online reports, multi-branch), the free SR Admin app, WhatsApp cortes and anti-fraud alerts (see 01). The incumbent can copy a thin version. **Likely.** [App Store listing via search](https://mwm.ai/apps/sr-admin/6575394645), [Cloudbeds](https://lobby.cloudbeds.com/qualified-bot/marketplace/soft-restaurant)
4. **ToastIQ is the proof the model works:** conversational assistant launched 2025-10-29; >50% of Toast locations used it within ~4 months, >1M queries; 40k weekly active users in Q1 2026. Paid agents sit on top (Grow from $699/mo, Team from $60/mo + $9/employee). **Verified.** [RTN](https://restauranttechnologynews.com/2025/10/toast-launches-conversational-ai-assistant-to-help-restaurant-operators-work-faster-and-smarter/), [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/toast-unveils-an-array-of-new-ai-agents-for-restaurants)
5. **Revenue sits in action agents (marketing, labor), not the chat assistant.** Chat is the free hook; Toast calls IQ Grow its fastest-growing product ever. Our margin-alert/price-suggestion is the cost-side analogue, and Toast is only teasing a pricing Menu Agent (early access). **Verified.**
6. **Costing tools (MarketMan, Apicbase, Restaurant365, xtraCHEF) are priced USD 170-500/location/month and need heavy setup - out of reach for MX independents.** Price sensitivity is the opening: MX POS runs about MXN 150-2,800/mo. **Verified** prices (third-party), **likely** conclusion.
7. **Toast and Square Restaurants appear not to be available in Mexico.** Toast: no MX launch found; Square: a comparison site says "not available in Mexico". **Likely.** Global entrants (Oracle Simphony, NCR Aloha) target chains. 
8. **The gap is real but narrow and defensible only on speed, price and data-access:** it is a wedge on Soft Restaurant's installed base, not a moat. If Polotab/Parrot add an AI layer, our edge reduces to "works with the POS you already have". Be honest about that.

## 3. The gap we can own

| Claim | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Owner-facing analytics for Soft Restaurant users, delivered on WhatsApp, at MX independent price levels | **Open as far as we found.** Closest: Kavasoft's CatIA (AI over SR data, cellar-focused) and FactorBI (SR dashboards, no AI). Entrants (Polotab, Parrot, Fudo) win by replacing the POS; costing tools (MarketMan, Apicbase, R365) are USD-priced, US/EU-centric and setup-heavy. |
| Margin-drift alerts + suggested price changes from ingredient cost moves | **Open.** Only Toast is teasing a pricing Menu Agent (early access, Oct 2026), US-only. Tight link to our core pain. |
| "No POS switch" positioning | Real: switching POS is costly for restaurants. But SR's installed base may not have clean recipe/cost data (see section C of brief) - **our hardest dependency**. |
| Moat | **Weak.** Data comes from SR report exports; Soft Restaurant could ship its own AI; Polotab/Parrot could add insights; Meta is building WhatsApp AI agents. Defensibility must come from trusted metrics, cost-capture workflow (CFDI, receipts), and being first to the installed base. |
| Biggest honest risk | The Phase 3 POS pits us against funded, moving competitors (Polotab YC, Parrot $11.7M). Phase 1-2 is a wedge, but Phase 3 is a different, crowded fight - unproven that analytics buyers convert to POS buyers. |
