## Notes on the top 5

1. **Manager upload (risk 1).** This is the single point of failure for the whole Phase 1 thesis. SR already has two automatic paths we did not plan for: the emailed Corte Z (SR 12) and the ERP/PMS JSON push (SR 11+, paid module, price **unverified**). Use the pilot to confirm both in week 1. If either works, the manager's daily job disappears and risks 1, 3 and 4 shrink together.
2. **Cost data (risk 5).** Without ingredient costs we are a nicer SR Admin app, which is free. Budget human time for recipe capture (about 4-6 hours per restaurant for the top-10 dishes, **assumption**) and charge a setup fee for it (see 10).
3. **Willingness to pay (risk 8).** Owners already pay SR MXN 800-1,300/month and see sales free in SR Admin. Sell the cost side in pesos ("este mes el bistec te comió $4,200 de margen"), and add waiter-level cancellation/discount leak patterns in pesos (SR 12 already sends per-event alerts, 14), which need no cost data and speak to a well-known owner fear (SR itself sells anti-fraud alerts [01]).
4. **Trust and wrong numbers (risks 6 and 7).** One wrong total in week 1 is unrecoverable. Code produces every number, the LLM only phrases it, and the first two weeks of each pilot get a human check of each batch. Reconcile every weekly report to the restaurant's corte.
5. **Capacity (risk 16).** Two devs cannot build parsers, an agent, costing, onboarding and sales in 4 weeks and also run pilots. One founder must own customers (onboarding, recipe capture, support) from week 1, even if that slows the build.
