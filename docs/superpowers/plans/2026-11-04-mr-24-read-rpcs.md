# MR-24 Remaining 7 read RPCs Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `get_dish_margin`, `get_food_cost_pct`, `get_sales_breakdown`, `get_cancellations`, `compare_locations`, `get_menu_engineering` and `get_cost_alerts` exist as SQL functions in schema `api`, answer in the C3 shapes (`packages/contracts/src/mermia_contracts/rpc.py`), tag every cost figure with its confidence, and pass pgTAP and contract tests on the El Güero seed.

**Architecture:** Two new migrations on top of MR-17 (`20261019200000_read_rpcs_first4.sql`) and the MR-25 costing engine (`20261022090000_costing_engine.sql`). Same pattern as MR-17: `SECURITY INVOKER` functions in `api`, helpers in `app`, the MR-17 envelope (`app.rpc_ok`, `app.rpc_error`, `app.flag`), periods (`app.resolve_period`), location scope (`app.scope_locations`), formatting and the unified-grain views. Every cost figure reads `dish_cost_snapshot`, which only `app.compute_dish_costs` writes; cost drivers re-walk the recipe of record with the engine's own helpers.

**Tech Stack:** Postgres 15 (Supabase), pgTAP, pytest + psycopg, Pydantic C3 models.

**Spec:** `docs/kickoff/09-architecture.md` §4.1; `docs/kickoff/03-data-ingestion-and-costing.md` (C.5 confidence, alert thresholds, Kasavana-Smith); `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-mr-11-contracts-rpc-tools-design.md` (D4, D23, D24); `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-mr-17-read-rpc-sources-decision.md`; `fixtures/README.md` (definitions).

## Global Constraints

- `packages/contracts/src` and the C3 examples are NOT changed (both devs approve contract changes). Where the contract is silent, take the most reasonable reading and list it in the PR under "Contract questions".
- Migrations are append-only: MR-17 and MR-25 functions are reused, not edited.
- Money is bigint centavos, percentages are percent points rounded once to four decimals at the end; no floats.
- RLS decides tenant and locations: every RPC is `SECURITY INVOKER`, an unseen location is `not_found`, and every RPC has a cross-tenant test.
- Tests compute snapshots with `app.compute_dish_costs` as the backend would, then call the RPC as `authenticated`.

## Decisions (open to change, repeated in the PR)

- **Confidence.** `get_dish_margin` returns the snapshot's `confidence`. The other cost answers have no field for it, so they carry exactly one flag: `cost_confirmed` or `cost_estimated` (the weakest snapshot used), plus `uncosted_sales` when some sales have no cost.
- **Target food cost** is 32.0 % (`app.food_cost_target()`, 03 "US Foods 32 %"): no column holds a per-tenant target and `tenant.settings` is backend-only. A breach is food cost > target + 3 pts (03 alert table).
- **Price moves:** a new price of record starting since the period start (through today) that moves at least 8 % against the price of record the day before, for ingredients used by a recipe in effect.
- **Dish matching:** an id, else the normalized name (lower case, accents removed); every word of the query must appear. One match resolves; several are `ambiguous` (top 5 by units sold in the last 30 days); none is `not_found`.
- **Dish location:** the asked location; with none, the one visible location that has a snapshot, or the first one when all agree on cost and price; otherwise `needs_location`.
- **compare_locations** ranks high to low, except `food_cost_pct` (low is best).
- **Menu engineering:** popularity ≥ 70 % of the equal share of units; contribution margin per unit against the units-weighted average; modifiers left out; only costed sales.
- **Sales breakdown:** `payment_method` reads payments on ticket days and `payment_mix` on aggregate days (tickets null there); `weekday` and `category` read the unified views; `staff`, `hour` and `channel` need ticket data.
- **Cancellations:** count and amount follow the day's grain (aggregate wins, rule C); top items and staff come from ticket records (voided lines and cancelled tickets).
